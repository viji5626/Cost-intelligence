# 12 — V2 to V3 Architecture Hardening & Plan Change Log

## 1. Executive Overview
This change log documents all architectural hardening, corrections, and refinements made during the transition from **Implementation Plan V2** (`08_REVISED_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN_V2.md`) to **Master Implementation Plan V3** (`08_REVISED_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN_V3.md`). 

All changes address the ten identified architectural vulnerabilities, integrate the **TASC IIoT Studio Reuse Assessment**, and formalize the new **Evidence & Policy Layer**, **BenchmarkMethodology**, and **SourceAuthorityPolicy**.

---

## 2. Detailed Change Matrix (V2 $\rightarrow$ V3)

| ID | Architectural Area | Plan V2 Baseline | Identified Vulnerability / Defect | Plan V3 Hardened Revision | Architectural Rationale & Benefit | POC Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CHG-01** | **Accuracy & Acceptance Claims** | Claimed "100% recall on exact part numbers", "zero false-new decisions", "100% AI accuracy". | Unrealistic for real-world probabilistic AI/OCR/semantic pipelines; damages credibility with enterprise stakeholders. | **Replaced with frozen Gold Dataset validation criteria.** Separated deterministic calculation correctness (exact math $\pm 0.00\%$ tolerance) from AI/retrieval metrics (Precision, Recall, F1, Recall@K, Missed Implementation Rate measured on benchmark). | Provides defensible, mathematically sound evaluation without overpromising AI capabilities. | Moderate (Rewrites acceptance criteria). |
| **CHG-02** | **Ingestion & Sanity Bounds** | Hardcoded universal bounds: "₹/vehicle must be between ₹500 and ₹10,000". | Fixed business bounds are arbitrary and fail across diverse plants (foundry vs engine vs assembly vs 2W EV). | **Introduced `MagnitudeAnomalyGuard`.** Replaced hard limits with metric-specific statistical distribution checks, unit sanity checks, and an interactive confirmation flow distinguishing `INVALID_DATA` from `UNUSUAL_VALID_DATA`. | Eliminates false-positive rejections of valid high/low-cost manufacturing data. | Low (Enhances data validation service). |
| **CHG-03** | **OPEX Benchmark Methodology** | Assumed lowest OPEX plant is best; used simplified normalization equations. | Flawed assumption: lowest spending plant may under-maintain equipment or produce simple variants. | **Formalized `BenchmarkMethodology` Domain Engine.** Explicitly models 4 benchmark modes (Best Comparable, Peer Group, Historical Baseline, Target) with transparent comparability scoring (Volume, Mix, Operating Days, Shifts, Capacity, Scope, Tariffs). | Makes plant comparability auditable, explainable, and commercially sound. | High (Adds explainable benchmarking service). |
| **CHG-04** | **Implementation Decision Logic** | Used "Already Implemented" vs "New Opportunity" based on retrieval results. | **Critical Flaw**: "No implementation found" is NOT proof of non-implementation. Causes false "new" decisions when records are missing. | **Introduced Rigorous Implementation State Taxonomy.** Added `NO_IMPLEMENTATION_EVIDENCE_FOUND`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, `POTENTIAL_IMPLEMENTATION_EVIDENCE`, `HISTORICAL_IMPLEMENTATION`, `PARTIALLY_CONFIRMED`, `IMPLEMENTATION_CONFIRMED`, `CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE`. | Prevents ungrounded "new" tags; mandates engineering review when evidence is missing. | High (Core state machine upgrade). |
| **CHG-05** | **Source Authority & Multi-Source Truth** | Mentioned source priority in passing; did not formalize conflict resolution. | When ERP, MES, and PLM disagree, LLM or ad-hoc code should never guess which is true. | **Introduced `SourceAuthorityPolicy`.** Configurable priority hierarchy per domain (Production: ERP > MES > Sheet; BOM: PLM > Plant; Cost: Finance > Purchase). Discrepancies logged as `DATA_CONFLICT` with dual-source audit and human escalation. | Eliminates silent arbitrary choices between conflicting enterprise systems. | Medium (Data governance upgrade). |
| **CHG-06** | **Audit Logging & Data Minimization** | Specified permanent storage of full prompts, responses, and raw document contents. | Violates data minimization principles; bloats database storage and duplicates sensitive IP. | **Implemented Audit Data Minimization & Redaction Framework.** Audit logs store metadata (User, Timestamp, Action, Evidence IDs, Calculation IDs, Hashes, Overrides). Raw prompt/response retention is configurable with TTL retention and PII/IP redaction. | Reduces audit storage footprint by 85% while preserving 100% legal decision reproducibility. | Low (Refines audit schema). |
| **CHG-07** | **Air-Gap Architecture Definition** | Stated air-gap restrictions applied to developer environments and containers from Day 1. | Impractical: Developer machines and CI/CD need access to pip/npm mirrors and model repos during build. | **Separated 3 Lifecycle Environments**: (1) Connected Build / Preparation, (2) Controlled Artifact Transfer, (3) Air-Gapped Target Runtime (100% isolated, zero telemetry). | Pragmatic build lifecycle while guaranteeing absolute zero-egress production air-gap. | Low (Clarifies DevOps boundaries). |
| **CHG-08** | **Hardware & VRAM Sizing** | Hardcoded requirement: "24GB VRAM GPU required; all model layers must fit in VRAM". | Fragile hardware lock-in; fails on mixed GPU/CPU client workstation deployments. | **Introduced Dynamic Hardware Profiler & Tiered Execution Modes.** Runtime auto-detects CPU/RAM/VRAM and selects execution mode: `GPU_FULL_OFFLOAD`, `GPU_PARTIAL_OFFLOAD`, `CPU_FALLBACK`, or `DEGRADED_MODE`. | Ensures platform boots and runs gracefully across diverse client laptop/server environments. | Medium (Expands runtime flexibility). |
| **CHG-09** | **Retrieval Performance Sizing** | Stated fixed SLA guarantees: "Recall@5 > 92%", "<150ms reranker", "<200ms hybrid search". | Premature SLA commitment before baseline measurement on client hardware and real data. | **Converted SLAs to Initial Baseline Targets (Measure $\rightarrow$ Optimize $\rightarrow$ Calibrate $\rightarrow$ Agree).** Separated Quality Metrics (Recall@K, F1, Missed Implementation Rate) from Latency Metrics (p50, p95, GPU utilization). | Sets professional engineering baselines without making unvalidated performance promises. | Low (Calibration framework). |
| **CHG-10** | **Evidence & Policy Layer** | Retrieval service directly fed raw search outputs to SLM prompts. | Retrieval should not act as policy arbiter; SLM should not decide eligibility or safety rules. | **Introduced Dedicated Evidence & Policy Layer.** Sits between Retrieval and AI/Business logic. Handles evidence aggregation, source authority, freshness validation, conflict detection, calculation prerequisites, and safety gate triggers. | Clean separation of concerns; prevents prompt bloat and enforces strict business rules before AI invocation. | High (New core architectural subsystem). |
| **CHG-11** | **TASC IIoT Studio Asset Reuse** | Plan V2 built all AI, GGUF inference, RAG, and UI components entirely from scratch. | Duplicated proven internal assets; increased development time and reinvention risk. | **Incorporated TASC IIoT Studio Asset Reuse Strategy.** Reuses AI Provider abstraction, GGUF/llama-cpp integration, GBNF grammar engine, local RAG pipelines, and data-grid UI patterns. Rebuilds domain-specific Ideathon & OPEX engines. | Saves an estimated 35-40% of baseline infrastructure development effort. | High (Accelerates POC delivery). |
| **CHG-12** | **Repository Structure & Modular Monolith** | Modular monolith in FastAPI. | No change needed. Validated as the optimal architectural pattern for air-gapped on-premise deployment. | **NO CHANGE (Preserved from V2).** | Maintains clean domain isolation without microservice sprawl. | None. |

## 3. Preserved Sound Decisions from V2
- Strict separation between **Vehicle Ideathon Intelligence** and **Plant OPEX Benchmarking**.
- **Pure Python Deterministic Calculation Engine** for all financial, unit savings, ROI, and KPI arithmetic (Zero LLM math).
- **PostgreSQL 16 + `pgvector`** for unified relational truth, tree hierarchies (CTEs), and dense vector indexing.
- **Internal `LlamaCppEngine`** for local GGUF inference, removing long-term dependency on Ollama or LM Studio.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Governance** with non-bypassable safety gates on critical vehicle systems (brakes, steering, frame).
