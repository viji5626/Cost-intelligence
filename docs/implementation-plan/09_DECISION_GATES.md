# 09 — Decision Gates & Quality Assurance Milestones

## 1. Decision Gate Framework Overview
Every phase of the **Hero Vehicle Cost & Plant OPEX Intelligence Platform** terminates in an explicit, non-bypassable Decision Gate. A phase cannot be marked complete, and downstream work cannot begin, until all Pass Criteria are verified and formally signed off.

---
